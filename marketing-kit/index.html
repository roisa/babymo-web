🎵TikTok 10 videos, ready to upload

The Studio videos and the 12 s Reels were made for Instagram. On TikTok they would likely be held back (shown to very few people) or flagged as unlabelled ads. These are made for TikTok: three real-app videos, four Baby Mo animations (“Pagi bareng Mo”, “3 syarat Mo”, “Mo vs. waktu tidur” and “Kalau ada tamu datang”, made from the render-3 poses) and three Baby Mo-only videos (“Menilai alasan anak”, “Cari Baby Mo dalam 3 detik” and “Kata Umi & Abi vs yang Mo dengar”), with what changed and how to post.

✕ Why the Studio videos and Reels can fail on TikTok

  • Silent audio track. The 12 s Reels carry a silent track (−91 dB). TikTok's For You feed favours videos with real sound; silent clips get very little reach.
  • Looks like an ad. Phone mockup, App Store badge and a “Download” pill read as an ad. Posted as an ordinary video without the commercial-content label, TikTok can limit or remove it.
  • Text where TikTok's own buttons sit. The “Unduh di App Store” pill sits in the bottom strip that TikTok covers with the caption and username.
  • Soft picture. The gameplay clips were recorded at 540 × 960 and scaled up. TikTok rates sharp 1080p video higher.
  • No hook in the first second. They open on a static title. Viewers decide in about one second, and early swipe-aways stop the video being shown to more people.

✓ What the new videos do instead

  • Real app footage at full 1080 × 1920, 30 fps (recorded in slow motion so it stays smooth), H.264 + AAC, about 5 MB each.
  • Real sound: Mo's greeting, the du'a and wudhu voices from the app, placed exactly where the app plays them, plus soft tap sounds. No music. Loudness about −14 LUFS, which is what TikTok expects.
  • Speaks to parents with a hook in the first second, in TikTok-style captions inside the safe area: clear of the top bar, the right-hand buttons and the caption strip.
  • Looks like a parent's screen recording, not an ad. Brand only in the last 3 s: Baby Mo + “Cari ‘Baby Mo’ di App Store”. No badge graphic, no price, no link.
  • 17–22 s: long enough to finish the moment, short enough that most people watch to the end.

Before you press Post

  1. Use a TikTok Business account. Business accounts can add the App Store link in the bio (TikTok doesn't make links in captions clickable).
  2. Before posting: More options → turn on “Content disclosure” → “Your brand”. It's our own app, so TikTok asks for this. Labelled videos still reach the For You feed.
  3. Turn on “AI-generated content” too: Mo's voice and the du'a recitation in the app are synthesised voices, and TikTok asks for a label on AI audio.
  4. Upload the MP4 as it is, without the TikTok editor's filters. Keep the original sound. If you add a sound, use only TikTok's Commercial Music Library, keep it under the voices, and post a no-music version too.
  5. Pick the cover: use the cover JPG or the first frame (it has the hook).
  6. Paste the caption below. Write to parents, not children: TikTok is 13+. Don't use #fyp spam. 3–5 specific hashtags work better.
  7. Post one video a day, 18:00–21:00 WIB. Reply to every comment in the first hour, and pin a comment such as “Cari Baby Mo di App Store 🍃”.
  8. Don't post the same file twice, and don't reupload Instagram Reels that carry an Instagram watermark.

🛡️Rules

✓ Do

  • Speak to parents and teachers. Children are the users; adults are the audience (ads must target adults only).
  • Use real app screens and the original Baby Mo renders (render 1). Render 2 Mo: upper body only.
  • Copy Arabic exactly from the app; ask a second person to check before posting.
  • Say “iPhone & iPad” (and Apple Watch where relevant): Baby Mo is not on Android.
  • Offer a no-music version of every Reel (voice + soft sound only): many families prefer it.
  • Get written consent before showing any child's face or voice; hands-only shots work well.
  • Use the official “Download on the App Store” badge from Apple's marketing resources for paid ads.

✕ Don't

  • Never picture Allah, the prophets or angels — talk about Isra Mi'raj and other events without depicting them.
  • No prices, “gratis/free” or discounts in posts and ads (the App Store shows the price per country).
  • No promises like “hafal dalam 3 hari” or medical/educational claims we can't prove.
  • No trending audio with unsuitable lyrics; no comparisons with other apps by name.
  • Don't ask children to comment, follow or DM; don't run contests aimed at children.
  • Don't share screenshots that show a real child's name entered in the app (use “Aisyah”).
