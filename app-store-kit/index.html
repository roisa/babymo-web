Baby Mo adalah teman belajar Islam untuk anak usia 2–8 tahun. Si kecil belajar doa, sholat, wudhu, dan huruf hijaiyah lewat 15 game yang seru — bersama Baby Mo, sahabat kecilnya. Tanpa iklan. Tanpa akun. Bisa dimainkan tanpa internet. BARU DI BABY MO • Tampilan baru yang ceria: langit, masjid, dan Baby Mo di setiap menu. • Satu suara yang jelas dan alami untuk semua bacaan Arab, Indonesia, dan Inggris — setelah dieja kata demi kata, doa dibaca utuh dengan suara alami. • Sahabat Hewan Mo — rawat kucing, ayam, kelinci, kambing, sapi, dan domba: beri makan, minum, bersihkan, dan elus dengan kasih sayang. • Kebun Kecil Ais — tanam, siram, cabut rumput liar, lalu panen tomat, stroberi, dan bunga matahari bersama Ais, dan belajar bersyukur serta berbagi. • Doa Harian kini berisi 14 doa dari pagi sampai malam, dengan cerita bergambar, baca bareng kata demi kata, dan stiker. • Huruf Hijaiyah lebih seru: menulis huruf, game Tangkap Huruf, dan Kotak Huruf. • Pulau Hijaiyah: huruf tersembunyi di peti harta, gua, pohon, dan balon, dengan hadiah setiap 4 huruf. • Akademi Tenis Kak Adam: latihan backhand dua tangan, pilihan gaya forehand, dan servis dari belakang garis. SEMUA YANG ADA DI BABY MO • Doa Harian — 14 doa dari bangun tidur sampai tidur lagi, dengan suara Arab, latin, dan arti. • Sholat Bareng Mo — panduan gerakan dan bacaan sholat, jadwal sholat hari ini, dan arah kiblat. • Wudhu Bareng Mo — 10 langkah wudhu lengkap dengan niat dan doa, plus game mengurutkan langkah. • Huruf Hijaiyah — 28 huruf Arab dalam 9 pelajaran bertahap, dengan suara, menulis, kuis, dan bintang. • Pulau Hijaiyah — jelajahi pulau kotak-kotak bersama Baby Mo dan temukan 28 huruf yang tersembunyi. • Kata Islam — susun huruf menjadi kata Arab pertama. • Akademi Tenis Kak Adam — 6 latihan dari bola merah sampai pertandingan pertama: tunggu pantulan, backhand dua tangan, forehand dan backhand, langkah kaki, servis, dan skor love-15-30-40. • Sahabat Hewan Mo — merawat hewan peternakan dengan adab dan kasih sayang. • Kebun Kecil Ais — berkebun dari benih sampai panen. • Dua Journey — cocokkan doa dengan momennya. • Memory Match — 7 level, dari 4 kartu untuk balita sampai 20 kartu untuk juara. • Cari Baby Mo — temukan Baby Mo yang bersembunyi di kamar, ruang keluarga, sampai pasar Ramadan. • Kenalan dengan Baby Mo — puzzle interaktif untuk perkenalan. • Kata Baik — puzzle kalimat thayyibah dan kata-kata yang baik. • Forehand Beat — game ritme yang seru. • Dreamtime — rutinitas tidur 4 langkah: gosok gigi, ganti baju, baca doa, lalu tidur. DIRANCANG UNTUK SI KECIL • Tombol besar yang mudah ditekan jari mungil • Suara lebih dulu, jadi anak yang belum bisa membaca tetap bisa bermain • Bintang hadiah di setiap game • Baby Mo menyapa si kecil dengan namanya • Mode terang dan gelap • Bahasa Indonesia dan Inggris AMAN UNTUK ANAK • Tanpa iklan dan tanpa pembelian dalam aplikasi • Tanpa akun, tanpa chat, tanpa fitur sosial • Tidak mengumpulkan data pribadi anak — nama dan progres hanya tersimpan di perangkat • Lokasi bersifat opsional, hanya untuk jadwal sholat dan kiblat; bisa memilih kota sendiri Doa, ayat, dan hadits disertai sumbernya. Baby Mo is an Islamic learning companion for children aged 2–8. Little ones learn du'as, prayer, wudu and the hijaiyah letters through 15 fun games — together with Baby Mo, their little friend. No ads. No account. Works offline. NEW IN BABY MO • A bright new look: sky, mosque and Baby Mo on every menu. • One clear, natural voice for all Arabic, Indonesian and English — after the word-by-word read-along, each du'a is read once in full. • Mo's Animal Friends — care for a cat, hen, rabbit, goat, cow and sheep: feed them, give them water, keep them clean and cuddle them kindly. • Ais's Little Garden — plant, water, pull the weeds, then harvest tomatoes, strawberries and sunflowers with Ais, while learning to be grateful and to share. • Daily Du'as now has 14 du'as from morning to night, with picture stories, word-by-word read-along and stickers. • Hijaiyah Letters is more playful: letter tracing, the Catch the Letters game and the Letter Box. • Hijaiyah Island: letters hidden in treasure chests, caves, trees and balloons, with a reward every 4 letters. • Kak Adam's Tennis Academy: a two-handed backhand drill, a choice of forehand style, and serving from behind the baseline. EVERYTHING INSIDE • Daily Du'as — 14 du'as from waking up to going back to sleep, with Arabic audio, transliteration and meaning. • Pray with Mo — a guide to the prayer movements and recitations, today's prayer times and the Qibla direction. • Wudu with Mo — the 10 steps of wudu with the intention and du'a, plus a sorting game. • Hijaiyah Letters — the 28 Arabic letters in 9 step-by-step lessons, with sound, tracing, quizzes and stars. • Hijaiyah Island — explore a blocky island with Baby Mo and find all 28 hidden letters. • Islamic Words — put letters together into first Arabic words. • Kak Adam's Tennis Academy — 6 drills from the red ball to a first match: wait for the bounce, two-handed backhand, forehand and backhand, footwork, serving and love-15-30-40 scoring. • Mo's Animal Friends — caring for farm animals with good manners and kindness. • Ais's Little Garden — gardening from seed to harvest. • Dua Journey — match each du'a to its moment. • Memory Match — 7 levels, from 4 cards for toddlers to 20 cards for champions. • Find Baby Mo — find Baby Mo hiding in his bedroom, the living room and even a Ramadan market. • Meet Baby Mo — an interactive getting-to-know-you puzzle. • Kind Words — a puzzle of good, kind words (kalimat thayyibah). • Forehand Beat — a fun rhythm game. • Dreamtime — a 4-step bedtime routine: brush teeth, change clothes, say the du'a, then sleep. DESIGNED FOR LITTLE ONES • Big buttons for small fingers • Sound first, so children who can't read yet can still play • Reward stars in every game • Baby Mo greets your child by name • Light and dark mode • Indonesian and English SAFE FOR CHILDREN • No ads and no in-app purchases • No accounts, no chat, no social features • No personal data collected from children — names and progress stay on the device • Location is optional and only used for prayer times and the Qibla; you can pick a city instead Du'as, verses and hadith come with their sources. Notes for App Review

Notes · App Review Information → Notes
Baby Mo is a free Islamic learning app for children aged 2–8 with 15 games: daily du'as, prayer and wudhu guides, Arabic letters, a letter-hunt island, a tennis-learning game, caring for farm animals and a little garden. There are no accounts, ads, in-app purchases, chat or social features. • No login is needed: open the app and choose a language. • Location is optional and only used for prayer times and the Qibla compass in the Prayer tab. If permission is denied, the user can pick a city by hand. Prayer times and the Qibla are calculated on the device; no location or other data ever leaves the device. • The child's nickname, stars and progress are stored only on the device. • Arabic read-aloud plays recordings bundled with the app (the device's speech voice is a fallback). There are no analytics or third-party SDKs, and every link out of the app and every share option is behind a parental gate (a maths question for grown-ups). • All games work offline. • The archive includes a small Apple Watch app: five daily du'as in the same recorded voice, a tasbih counter, on-device prayer times (the parent picks a city by hand — the watch never uses location), Ramadan countdowns and a simple pet check-in. Like the iPhone app it is fully offline, has no accounts and collects no data. Privacy policy: https://babymo.id/privacy/ · Support: https://babymo.id/support/

Heads-up

Note · "File dimensions are invalid" means the image doesn't match the slot. Check the sizes listed under the upload box: the iPhone slot (Dynamic Island, medium display) takes 1206 × 2622, the iPad 13" slot takes 2064 × 2752 (or the 2048 × 2732 fallback set). The iPhone Duo slot takes 2007 × 2853. Drop iPhone files only on the iPhone tab, Duo files only on the iPhone Duo tab, and iPad files only on the iPad tab.
Note · Kids Category: the iOS build has a parental gate (a grown-up sum) in front of every link that leaves the app and every share button, collects no data and calls no third-party services, so it meets Guideline 1.3. Paste the review notes above when you re-submit.
Note · iPad: the app is portrait-only (iPhone and iPad) with UIRequiresFullScreen, and on iPad every game is scaled to fill the screen. The iPad screenshots were captured that way. If you later want landscape on iPad, remove UIRequiresFullScreen and add the landscape orientations back in Info.plist — and check the layouts first.
Note · Every new build needs a higher build number than the one Apple already has; Xcode bumps it on upload. Before archiving run git pull and npx cap sync ios so the app has the latest games.
