Ramadan Habits to Do With Kids

Warm ways to grow a love of Ramadan from a young age

When & how

Throughout the month of Ramadan — little by little, suited to the child's age and ability.

For your little one

Some joyful habits to share: waking for sahur together, decorating a Ramadan countdown, saving for a daily charity, joining a few rak'ah of Tarawih, adding one memorized surah, and hearing one good-character story before bed. What matters isn't the quantity but the joy — so the child looks forward to next Ramadan.
