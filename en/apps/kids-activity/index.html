How it works

Frequently asked questions

Is this worksheet generator free?

Yes, completely free. There's no cost, no account, and no limit on how many worksheets you can generate and print.

Do I need to sign up or log in?

No. Everything runs right in your browser. There's no sign-up and no data is sent to a server.

What ages are the worksheets for?

There are three levels: ages 3–4, 5–6, and 7–8. The age level adjusts the difficulty of each activity.

Can I print or save as PDF?

Yes. Download the whole pack as a single PDF, save each sheet as PDF or SVG, or use the Print button straight from your browser.

Is it good for teachers and homeschooling?

Absolutely. Generate many sheets at once for a class, or mix several activities into one practice pack.
