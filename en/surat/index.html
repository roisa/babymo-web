Juz 'Amma for Children

Short Surahs of the Qur'an

15 short surahs from Juz 30 — the ones we recite most in prayer and teach children first. Each surah includes Arabic text, transliteration, translation, and child-friendly tafsir.

Surah 114

An-Nas

Mankind · 6 verses · Meccan

النَّاس

The very last surah of the Qur'an. Together with Al-Falaq, recited before sleep to seek refuge in Allah.

Read in full →

Surah 113

Al-Falaq

The Daybreak · 5 verses · Meccan

الْفَلَق

Surah 113 — companion to An-Nas. Seeks refuge in Allah from the evil of anything He has created.

Read in full →

Surah 112

Al-Ikhlas

The Purity of Faith · 4 verses · Meccan

الْإِخْلَاص

Four short verses that answer the most fundamental question: who is Allah? The Prophet ﷺ said its value equals one-third of the Qur'an.

Read in full →

Surah 111

Al-Lahab

The Flame / The Palm Fiber · 5 verses · Meccan

الْمَسَد

A story about an uncle of the Prophet who opposed his message. Lesson: wealth doesn't save us in the hereafter.

Read in full →

Surah 110

An-Nasr

The Help · 3 verses · Medinan

النَّصْر

The last surah revealed in full — a sign of the Prophet's approaching passing, and a promise of Islam's victory.

Read in full →

Surah 109

Al-Kafirun

The Disbelievers · 6 verses · Meccan

الْكَافِرُون

A surah about firm faith without hostility. Teaches: we worship our way, they worship theirs — without coercion.

Read in full →

Surah 108

Al-Kautsar

Abundance · 3 verses · Meccan

الْكَوْثَر

The shortest surah in the Qur'an — only 3 verses. Contains the command to pray and sacrifice as proof of gratitude.

Read in full →

Surah 107

Al-Ma'un

Small Kindnesses · 7 verses · Meccan

الْمَاعُون

A surah linking the quality of prayer with social compassion. Prayer without caring for others = incomplete prayer.

Read in full →

Surah 106

Quraysh

The Tribe of Quraysh · 4 verses · Meccan

قُرَيْش

About the blessings of safety and provision Allah gave the Quraysh tribe — and their duty to be grateful.

Read in full →

Surah 105

Al-Fil

The Elephant · 5 verses · Meccan

الْفِيل

The story of Abrahah's elephant army that tried to destroy the Ka'bah — defeated by small birds Allah sent.

Read in full →

Surah 104

Al-Humazah

The Slanderer · 9 verses · Meccan

الْهُمَزَة

A stern warning against two evil traits: backbiting others and hoarding wealth stingily.

Read in full →

Surah 103

Al-Asr

Time · 3 verses · Meccan

الْعَصْر

Three verses Imam Shafi'i considered a summary of the entire Qur'an. The way to live without loss.

Read in full →

Surah 102

At-Takathur

Rivalry in Worldly Increase · 8 verses · Meccan

التَّكَاثُر

A warning against obsession with collecting wealth and status until we forget the afterlife.

Read in full →

Surah 101

Al-Qari'ah

The Striking Calamity · 11 verses · Meccan

الْقَارِعَة

A depiction of the Day of Judgement — and the scales of deeds that determine everyone's fate.

Read in full →

Surah 100

Al-'Adiyat

The Racers · 11 verses · Meccan

الْعَادِيَات

Allah swears by galloping war horses — then reminds people not to forget to thank their Lord.

Read in full →

Surah 99

Az-Zalzalah

The Earthquake · 8 verses · Medinan

الزَّلْزَلَة

About the Day of Judgement — Allah's promise: the smallest good is rewarded, the smallest bad is seen.

Read in full →

Surah 97

Al-Qadr

The Power / Decree · 5 verses · Meccan

الْقَدْر

About Lailatul Qadr — one night in Ramadan better than 1000 months. The night the Qur'an first descended.

Read in full →

Surah 96

Al-Alaq

The Clot · 19 verses · Meccan

الْعَلَق

These are the very first verses revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ in the Cave of Hira — beginning with one word: "Read!" A surah about knowledge and the honor of reading.

Read in full →

Surah 95

At-Tin

The Fig · 8 verses · Meccan

التِّين

Allah created humans in the best form — and reminds us to keep that goodness through faith and good deeds.

Read in full →

Surah 94

Al-Inshirah (Ash-Sharh)

The Relief · 8 verses · Meccan

الشَّرْح

A surah full of hope: after hardship comes ease. Repeated twice so we're sure.

Read in full →

Surah 93

Ad-Duha

The Morning Hours · 11 verses · Meccan

الضُّحَى

Revealed to comfort the Prophet ﷺ when he was sad — Allah never left him, and won't leave us.

Read in full →

Surah 91

Ash-Shams

The Sun · 15 verses · Meccan

الشَّمْس

This surah opens with Allah swearing by the sun, moon, day, night, sky, and earth — then invites us to keep our hearts pure.

Read in full →

Surah 87

Al-A'la

The Most High · 19 verses · Meccan

الْأَعْلَى

A surah that is easy to memorize and loved in prayer. It calls us to glorify the name of our Lord, the Most High.

Surah 1

Al-Fatihah

The Opening · 7 verses · Meccan

الْفَاتِحَة

The opening chapter of the Qur'an. Recited in every unit of prayer — the verses we say most often in our lives.
