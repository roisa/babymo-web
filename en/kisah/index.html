25 Prophets · For Children

Stories of the 25 Prophets

From Prophet Adam, created from earth, to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, who united the Arabian peninsula in 23 years. Each story is brief yet meaningful — with three core lessons we can teach our children today.

Prophet 1

Prophet Adam

The first human

آدَم

Created from earth, honored with knowledge, then sent to earth after being tested.

Read story →

Prophet 2

Prophet Idris

After Prophet Adam

إِدْرِيس

The first prophet to learn writing — raised by Allah to a lofty station.

Read story →

Prophet 3

Prophet Nuh (Noah)

After generations of Adam-Idris drifted from tawhid

نُوح

Preached for 950 years with patience — then built a great ark by Allah's command.

Read story →

Prophet 4

Prophet Hud

Sent to the 'Ad — a tribe of giants in southern Arabia

هُود

Warned a strong, tall people against arrogance — ignored, then destroyed by wind.

Read story →

Prophet 5

Prophet Salih

Sent to the Thamud — descendants of the 'Ad

صَالِح

Asked for one miracle: a she-camel from rock. His people killed it — and were destroyed.

Read story →

Prophet 6

Prophet Ibrahim (Abraham)

Father of the prophets — ancestor of Ismail and Ishaq

إِبْرَاهِيم

Questioned his father's idols, was thrown into fire, then built the Ka'bah with Ismail.

Read story →

Prophet 7

Prophet Lut (Lot)

Nephew of Ibrahim — sent to Sodom

لُوط

Warned his people from grave immorality — they refused, and the city was overturned.

Read story →

Prophet 8

Prophet Ismail (Ishmael)

Eldest son of Ibrahim — ancestor of the Arabs and Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

إِسْمَاعِيل

Left as a child in the valley of Makkah — became the source of zam-zam and a holy city.

Read story →

Prophet 9

Prophet Ishaq (Isaac)

Son of Ibrahim by Sarah — ancestor of the Children of Israel

إِسْحَاق

Born to a mother in old age — proof Allah is over what seems impossible.

Read story →

Prophet 10

Prophet Ya'qub (Jacob)

Son of Ishaq — father of Yusuf and his 11 brothers

يَعْقُوب

Lost his sight from weeping over Yusuf — vision restored when Yusuf's shirt touched his face.

Read story →

Prophet 11

Prophet Yusuf (Joseph)

11th son of Ya'qub — his story is an entire surah

يُوسُف

Thrown into a well, sold as a slave, imprisoned — then became ruler of Egypt and saved his family.

Read story →

Prophet 12

Prophet Ayyub (Job)

Lived in the land of Uz, around early Children of Israel times

أَيُّوب

Tested with loss of wealth, children, and health — kept giving thanks until restored.

Read story →

Prophet 13

Prophet Syu'aib (Shuaib)

Sent to Madyan — a trading city full of fraud

شُعَيْب

Called his people to honest trade — ignored, they were destroyed by an earthquake.

Read story →

Prophet 14

Prophet Musa (Moses)

Ancient Egypt, era of Pharaoh

مُوسَى

A baby set adrift, raised in his enemy's palace, then led the Children of Israel out of Egypt.

Read story →

Prophet 15

Prophet Harun (Aaron)

Older brother of Musa — partner in mission

هَارُون

Accompanied Musa facing Pharaoh, and guarded the Children of Israel when Musa went to Sinai.

Read story →

Prophet 16

Prophet Dzulkifli

After Prophet Ayyub's era

ذُو الْكِفْل

Volunteered for a heavy task — three promises: fasting, qiyam, and not getting angry while ruling.

Read story →

Prophet 17

Prophet Daud (David)

King of the Children of Israel — recipient of the Zabur

دَاوُد

Defeated the giant Jalut (Goliath) with a sling, then became a just king whose voice calmed mountains.

Read story →

Prophet 18

Prophet Sulaiman (Solomon)

Son of Daud — a kingdom unlike any before

سُلَيْمَان

Given dominion over jinn, animals, and the wind — yet called himself 'a servant who returns much to Allah.'

Read story →

Prophet 19

Prophet Ilyas (Elijah)

Children of Israel after Sulaiman — when they worshipped the idol Ba'al

إِلْيَاس

Called his people away from worshipping the idol Ba'al — ignored, then Allah tested them with drought.

Read story →

Prophet 20

Prophet Ilyasa' (Elisha)

Successor of Prophet Ilyas's mission

الْيَسَع

Loyal student of Ilyas who continued the mission after his teacher's passing.

Read story →

Prophet 21

Prophet Yunus (Jonah)

Sent to Nineveh — a great city of Assyria

يُونُس

Angrily left his people — swallowed by a great fish — prayed inside the fish and was rescued.

Read story →

Prophet 22

Prophet Zakariya (Zachariah)

Guardian of Maryam at Bayt al-Maqdis — father of Prophet Yahya

زَكَرِيَّا

Prayed for a child in extreme old age — answered with Prophet Yahya.

Read story →

Prophet 23

Prophet Yahya (John the Baptist)

Son of Zakariya — cousin of Prophet Isa

يَحْيَى

Born to a barren mother and aged father — grew into a young man who never sinned.

Read story →

Prophet 24

Prophet Isa (Jesus)

Born of the pure virgin Maryam — sent to the Children of Israel

عِيسَى

Born without a father, spoke from the cradle, performed many miracles — raised alive to heaven by Allah.

Read story →

Prophet 25

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

The final prophet — sent to all humanity until the end of time

مُحَمَّد

Orphaned young, trusted with the title 'Al-Amin,' received the first revelation at Cave Hira, and united Arabia in 23 years.

Read story →
