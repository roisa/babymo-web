Free Kids Activity Worksheet Generator: Make & Print in Seconds

Written by Tim Baby Mo·2026-06-23·5 min read

Need a quick activity for your little one — without buying an activity book or subscribing to anything? Baby Mo's Kids Activity Worksheet Generator makes printable worksheets in seconds. Pick a theme, choose an age, select the activity types, hit Generate — done.

✨ Open the Kids Activity Generator

Why parents love it

  • Free & no sign-up. No cost, no account, no limit on how many sheets you make.
  • Runs in your browser. Nothing to install; no data sent anywhere.
  • Print-ready. Download a single PDF pack, save per sheet (PDF/SVG), or print directly.
  • Age-appropriate. Three levels — 3–4, 5–6, and 7–8 — that adjust the difficulty.

9 activity types in one place

🌀 Mazes · ✏️ Tracing · 🎨 Coloring · 🔗 Matching · 🧩 Puzzles · 🔎 Word Search · 🔢 Count & Write · ➡️ What Comes Next · 🔵 Dot-to-Dot. Eight themes: Dinosaurs, Space, Animals, Princess, Vehicles, Alphabet, Ramadan, and Ocean.

Real, everyday use cases

  • Rainy day at home. Print a maze + coloring pack and your child is happily busy, screen-free.
  • Waiting at a restaurant. Keep a word search or dot-to-dot in your bag — a lifesaver while food arrives.
  • Travel & school holidays. Prep a few sheets to do in the car or at the hotel.
  • Homeschooling. Mix letter tracing, counting, and patterns into one daily practice pack.
  • For teachers. Set 1–10 sheets per activity to cover a whole class in one go.
  • Islamic moments. Choose the Ramadan theme for sheets that feel special.

How it works — 3 steps

  1. Pick a theme & age. Eight themes, three age levels.
  2. Choose activities. Tick one or more, set how many sheets each.
  3. Generate & print. Hit Generate, then download PDF, save SVG, or print.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the activity worksheet generator free?+
Yes, completely free — no cost, no account, and no limit on how many sheets you can make and print.
Do I need to sign up or install an app?+
No. Everything runs in your browser and no data is sent to a server.
What ages are the worksheets for?+
Three levels: ages 3–4, 5–6, and 7–8, with difficulty that adjusts to each.
Can I print or save as PDF?+
Yes. Download the whole pack as one PDF, save each sheet as PDF/SVG, or print directly from the browser.
Is it good for teachers and homeschooling?+
Very much. Make many sheets at once for a class, or mix activities into one practice pack.
