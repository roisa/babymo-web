21:T4e6,<p>Rabi' al-Akhir is the fourth month of the <a href="/en/blog/12-bulan-hijriah-untuk-anak/">Hijri calendar</a>. Unlike Ramadan or Dhul-Hijjah, this month has no festival or special worship. Yet this "quiet month" holds a precious lesson for children.</p>

<h2>The best deeds are the consistent ones</h2>
<p>The Prophet ﷺ said: <em>"The deeds most beloved to Allah are the most consistent, even if small."</em> <strong>(Bukhari &amp; Muslim.)</strong> A month with no major day is the perfect time to train istiqamah — small daily obedience that never stops.</p>

<h2>Small habits a child can keep</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Reading the <a href="/en/doa/doa-pagi/">morning</a> &amp; <a href="/en/doa/doa-petang/">evening</a> remembrance daily.</li>
  <li>One line of memorizing a <a href="/en/surat/">short surah</a>.</li>
  <li>Helping with one chore without being asked.</li>
  <li>Saying Bismillah before eating and Alhamdulillah after.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Tips for parents</h2>
<p>Pick one habit, not ten. Make a simple checklist board, and celebrate each "tick" as a small win. See <a href="/en/blog/kebiasaan-kecil-yang-tumbuh-besar/">small habits that grow big</a> for ideas.</p>
