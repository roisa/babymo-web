<h2>An easy 30-day plan</h2>

<div class="rm-plan">
<div><h3>🌙 Days 1–10 · Getting started</h3><p>The crescent du‘a, the intention to fast and “I am fasting”. Read books 1–3. For toddlers, try fasting until 10 am or until Dhuhr.</p></div>
<div><h3>🥁 Days 11–20 · Building habits</h3><p>The iftar du‘a every Maghrib, sharing iftar treats, one hijaiyah letter a day, and joining tarawih even for a few rakaat. Read books 4–7.</p></div>
<div><h3>✨ Days 21–30 · Special nights</h3><p>The Laylatul Qadr du‘a before bed, preparing Zakat al-Fitr together, then the takbir and Eid greeting. Read books 8–10.</p></div>

<details><summary>Does my child need to be fasting?</summary><p>No. Children are not obliged to fast before puberty. The series introduces the atmosphere and du‘as of Ramadan joyfully — practice fasts can start gently whenever your child is ready.</p></details>
