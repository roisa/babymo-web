An easy 30-day plan

🌙 Days 1–10 · Getting started

The crescent du‘a, the intention to fast and “I am fasting”. Read books 1–3. For toddlers, try fasting until 10 am or until Dhuhr.

🥁 Days 11–20 · Building habits

The iftar du‘a every Maghrib, sharing iftar treats, one hijaiyah letter a day, and joining tarawih even for a few rakaat. Read books 4–7.

✨ Days 21–30 · Special nights

The Laylatul Qadr du‘a before bed, preparing Zakat al-Fitr together, then the takbir and Eid greeting. Read books 8–10.

The Prophet ﷺ said: “Whoever gives food to a fasting person to break their fast will have a reward like theirs, without it reducing their reward in the slightest.” (Tirmidhi 807)

Frequently asked questions

What age is it for?

Roughly 2–8. Toddlers listen and tap; older children can read along with the transliteration and learn the meanings.

Does my child need to be fasting?

No. Children are not obliged to fast before puberty. The series introduces the atmosphere and du‘as of Ramadan joyfully — practice fasts can start gently whenever your child is ready.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Ramadan Du‘as and the Twins’ Ramadan stories can be tried right in the browser. The app brings everything together, works offline and adds Apple Watch.

What about the Arabic texts?

The Arabic is fully voweled, with the hadith source shown for every du‘a. The intention wordings follow those commonly taught in Indonesia.
