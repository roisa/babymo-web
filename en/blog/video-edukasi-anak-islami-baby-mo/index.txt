<h2>1. Daily duas for kids</h2>
<p>Short videos (30–45 seconds) that teach one dua through Baby Mo’s adorable antics. Being short, they make perfect <strong>routine cues</strong> — play one whenever the moment arrives.</p>
<ul>
  <li><a href="/en/blog/lagu-doa-sebelum-tidur-anak/">Dua Before Sleeping</a> — a calming close to the day.</li>
  <li><a href="/en/blog/lagu-doa-bangun-tidur-anak/">Dua Upon Waking</a> — open the day with gratitude.</li>
  <li><a href="/en/blog/lagu-doa-sebelum-makan-anak/">Dua Before Eating</a> and <a href="/en/blog/lagu-doa-sesudah-makan-anak/">After Eating</a> — table manners.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>The benefit:</strong> planting small, consistent acts of worship. A dua read daily slowly becomes automatic — and good habits are the foundation of character.</p>

<h2>2. Islamic kids’ songs</h2>
<p>Melody and repetition help kids memorize far faster than rote learning, while building vocabulary.</p>
<ul>
  <li><a href="/en/blog/lagu-bismillah-dulu/">Bismillah Dulu</a> — begin everything with Allah’s name.</li>
  <li><a href="/en/blog/lagu-hujan-turun-rintik-rintik/">Hujan Turun Rintik-Rintik</a> — a warm song for parent-child bonding.</li>
</ul>
