<h2>What children do</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>9 step-by-step lessons</strong> — from alif, ba, ta, tha all the way to ya.</li>
  <li><strong>Listen</strong> to each letter’s sound and an example word.</li>
  <li><strong>Trace</strong> the letter with a finger along the dots.</li>
  <li><strong>Letter Catch</strong> — pick the balloon with the letter you hear, then a little quiz and stars.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What it builds</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Recognising the shapes and sounds of the 28 letters.</li>
  <li>Fine motor skills through tracing.</li>
  <li>Listening for sounds that are close to each other.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Tips for parents</h2>

<ul>
  <li>One lesson a day is plenty — repeat it tomorrow before moving on.</li>
  <li>Say the sounds together, then find the same letter in a mushaf or iqra book.</li>
  <li>Celebrate every new letter; don’t wait until they know them all.</li>
</ul>
