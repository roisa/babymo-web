Privacy Policy

Baby Mo is an Islamic learning world for young children and their families: the website babymo.id, the games at play.babymo.id, and the Baby Mo app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to information when your family uses Baby Mo.

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no login.
  • No advertising, no tracking, and we never sell or share data.
  • We do not collect personal information from children.
  • Things like your child's nickname, stickers and game progress stay on your device — they are never sent to us.

1. Information Baby Mo collects

We do not collect personal information. We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, birthdays, photos, voice recordings, contacts or payment details, and we do not run a database of users.

To make Baby Mo work, some small settings are saved only on your own device (in the browser's local storage or the app's storage):

  • a first name or nickname for your child, only if you type one in (for example "This book is read for…" or the greeting in the games);
  • stickers collected, fact cards opened, game scores and progress, completed missions and prayer check-ins;
  • preferences such as language, light/dark theme, sound on/off, reading speed and chosen voice;
  • bookmarks and saved items;
  • the location or city you chose for prayer times (see section 4).

This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us.

2. Children's personal information

Baby Mo is made for children aged about 2–8 to use together with a parent. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. There is no chat, no messaging, no profiles, no photos or video uploads, no social features, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

If you type your child's nickname, it is stored only on your device to personalise the stories and games, and it is never transmitted. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

3. Analytics and third-party services

The Baby Mo app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not use Apple's advertising identifier and we do not track users across other companies' apps or websites.

The services below may receive limited technical information. We have chosen privacy-respecting options and none of them receive your child's name or any account details:

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages). babymo.id and play.babymo.id are static websites hosted by GitHub. Like any web server, GitHub may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security. We do not receive or use these logs.
  • Website analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics, babymo.id only). If enabled, this counts page views on the babymo.id website in aggregate. It uses no cookies, does not fingerprint visitors and does not identify anyone. The games at play.babymo.id and the iOS app contain no analytics at all.
  • Prayer times. Prayer times (Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs parameters) and the Qibla direction are calculated entirely on your device. No coordinates or other data are sent to any service, and no city-name lookup is used.
  • Videos (YouTube). Some pages on babymo.id show Baby Mo videos using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube may collect information when a video is played, under Google's privacy policy. The iOS app does not embed YouTube.
  • Read-aloud voices. Arabic letters, du'as and words are played from recordings bundled with the app and website; anything without a recording is read by your device's built-in speech engine. Baby Mo does not record or send audio, and the app does not use the microphone.
  • Links for parents. Pages for parents may link to WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or donation pages (Saweria, Trakteer, PayPal). These open only if you tap them, and those sites have their own privacy policies. In the iOS app, every link that leaves the app and every share option is behind a parental gate (a question for grown-ups).

Fonts, images, sounds and code are served from our own websites or bundled inside the app.

4. Location

Location is optional and is used only to show prayer times and the Qibla direction. The app asks for permission ("While Using the App") before using it, and you can say no and choose a city by hand instead. The location is saved only on your device, and the Qibla direction is calculated on the device. You can turn location off at any time in your device settings (on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Baby Mo).

5. How children's information is protected

  • Data minimisation: we designed Baby Mo so it does not need personal information at all.
  • On-device storage: nicknames, progress and settings are kept on your device, not on our servers.
  • No ads, no profiling, no tracking, and no selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • Encrypted connections: our websites and the services above use HTTPS.
  • Nothing leaves the device: location used for prayer times and the Qibla never leaves your device.
  • Parental gate: in the iOS app, links out of the app and sharing require a grown-up to answer a question first.
  • Parents in control: you can clear everything at any time (see section 7).

6. Where information is stored

Information is stored locally on your device. Baby Mo has no user accounts and no server database, so we have no personal information about you or your child on our servers. Our servers only deliver the website files, pictures and games.

7. Deleting data

Because information is stored on your device, you can delete it yourself at any time:

  • iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch app: delete the Baby Mo app — all its saved data is removed with it.
  • Websites: clear the site data for babymo.id and play.babymo.id in your browser settings (for example in Safari: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data).

You can also email us at roisa.mscdr@icloud.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will reply within 14 days and delete any information we hold about you or your child, such as emails you have sent us. Parents can also ask us what information, if any, we hold.

8. Your rights

We follow Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27 Tahun 2022) and the principles of children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States) and the GDPR (European Union). You may ask to access, correct or delete any personal data about you or your child by contacting us.

9. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact the Baby Mo team (Ibra Creative, Indonesia):
Email: roisa.mscdr@icloud.com
Website: babymo.id

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Baby Mo handles information, we will update this page and the date below before the change takes effect. We will never start collecting children's personal information without first updating this policy.
