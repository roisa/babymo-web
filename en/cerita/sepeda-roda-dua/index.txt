Abi sat beside him. They were quiet for a while, watching a bird fly over the tree.

Baby Mo wiped his tears. "A thousand times?"

Baby Mo raised his hands. "Ya Allah, please help me ride my bike. Aamiin."

Abi taught him a little line: "Fall down once, get up again. Bismillah, try again!"

Baby Mo stood up. He put his helmet back on. He climbed on the bike. "Bismillah," he whispered.

He pedalled. Abi held the saddle and ran beside him. The bike wobbled… but Baby Mo kept pedalling. Straight. Faster. Even straighter.

"Abi, don't let go!" shouted Baby Mo.

But Abi had let go a while ago. Abi was standing far behind, clapping.

Baby Mo was riding all by himself! The wind blew on his cheeks. Whoooosh! He laughed out loud.

"I CAN DO IT! ABI, I CAN DO IT!"
