That noon, Baby Mo saw the ball on the table. "Just borrowing it for a bit," he whispered. He took it to the garden.

He bounced it really high. Bonk… bonk… BONK! The ball flew up, then dropped into the bushes.

Baby Mo searched the bushes. Nothing. Under the pots. Nothing. His heart began to thump.

That afternoon Kak Adam shouted, "My ball's gone! Has anyone seen my lucky ball?"

Everyone joined the search. Abi under the sofa. Baby Ais with her magnifying glass. Baby Mo… stayed quiet in the corner.

"Mo, have you seen it?" asked Kak Adam. Baby Mo shook his head fast. "No, Kak." But his tummy suddenly ached.

That night Baby Mo couldn't sleep. That word "no" felt heavy, like a stone in his tummy.

Abi came in and sat on the edge of the bed. "You look worried, Mo. Is there something you want to tell me?"

Baby Mo took a breath, and it all came out: "I took the ball… it got lost in the bushes… and then I said no…"

Abi wasn't cross. He hugged him. "Thank you for being honest, Mo. Honesty is hard, but you were brave."

They crawled under the bushes, with Baby Ais and her magnifying glass. Then… "FOUND IT! In a flowerpot!"
