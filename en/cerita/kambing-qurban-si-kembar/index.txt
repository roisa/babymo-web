33:T8d5,A week before Eid al-Adha, a special guest arrived in the garden: a white goat! "Maaa-aaah!"

"This is our qurban goat," said Abi. Mo named him straight away. "His name is… Si Embek!"

Every morning, the twins gave Si Embek grass and water. Si Embek chewed… munch, munch… blinking his eyes.

"Abi, why do we do qurban?" asked Ais one afternoon.

Abi told them about Prophet Ibrahim, peace be upon him, who obeyed Allah completely. Allah tested him with a very hard command, and he was ready to obey. Then Allah sent a great ram in its place.

> *"And We ransomed him with a great sacrifice." (Qur'an, As-Saffat 37:107)*

"So qurban is a sign that we obey Allah," said Abi. "And a way to share meat with lots of people."

On the night before Eid al-Adha, Mo sat hugging his knees beside Si Embek. "I love Si Embek…"

Abi sat beside him. "Loving animals is good, Mo. The Prophet ﷺ taught us to be kind to animals — even when slaughtering them."

> *"Allah has prescribed excellence in everything. When you slaughter, slaughter well: sharpen the blade and put the animal at ease." (Muslim 1955)*

"Won't Si Embek be hurt?" "The helpers do it quickly and calmly, saying Allah's name," said Abi gently. Mo nodded slowly.

On the morning of Eid al-Adha, the takbir rang out again. The whole family prayed Eid at the field, then came home a different way.

At midday, the qurban team at the mosque was very busy. Kak Adam helped pack the meat into little woven boxes.

Abi explained, "We eat some of the meat, keep some, and give some in charity to people in need."

> *About the qurban meat, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Eat, store some, and give in charity." (Muslim 1971)*

Mo and Ais delivered the boxes from house to house. "Assalamu'alaikum! Here's some qurban meat for you, Nenek!" The old lady smiled. "Alhamdulillah, I haven't had meat for so long."

Mo went quiet. Now he understood: Si Embek had made so many people happy today.

That night Abi said, "Remember, it isn't the meat or the blood that reaches Allah, but our devotion." Mo hugged Abi tight.

> *"Neither their meat nor their blood reaches Allah, but what reaches Him is your devotion." (Qur'an, Al-Hajj 22:37)*
