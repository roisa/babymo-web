36:T674,Abi had a black watch. Every day he glanced at it — glance, glance, glance.

That noon Abi was typing on his laptop. Tap-tap-tap-tap. Then he glanced at his watch… and stood straight up!

In the afternoon Abi was on the phone. "Yes… certainly…" Glance at the watch — "Sorry, I'll call you back." Click!

"Abi's watch must be MAGIC!" thought Baby Mo. "It can make Abi stop!"

Baby Mo got Baby Ais to investigate. They hid behind the sofa with a magnifying glass.

They watched. Every time Abi stopped, the same sound came from far away: "Allaahu Akbar, Allaahu Akbar…"

"Abi! We know the secret!" said the twins. "Your watch plays the adhan!" Abi laughed.

"It's just an ordinary watch," said Abi. "I look at it so I know when prayer time is coming. Each prayer has its own time."

> *"Indeed, prayer has been decreed upon the believers at fixed times." (Qur'an, An-Nisa 4:103)*

"Fajr is when the sky is still dark. Dhuhr is when the sun is overhead. Asr is when shadows grow long…"

"…Maghrib is when the sun sets, and Isha is when the stars come out."

"When the adhan comes, however important my work is, I stop first. Because it's Allah calling."

"I want a watch too!" said Baby Mo. Abi took a marker and drew a watch on his wrist.

From that day, Baby Mo became the Prayer-Time Keeper. Whenever he heard the adhan, he shouted, "PRAYER TIME!"

Then he stopped playing, answered the adhan softly, and ran to fetch his little prayer mat.

Abi smiled proudly. "The magic isn't the watch — it's a heart that's ready to stop when Allah calls."
