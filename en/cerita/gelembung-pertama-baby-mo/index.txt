34:T7a1,"Tomorrow is the first swimming lesson!" said Abi. Ais bounced with joy. But Mo stayed quiet, hugging his towel.

"I'm scared," he whispered. "The water will go up my nose. I'll sink."

Abi didn't say "don't be scared". He took Mo to the swimming-pool playroom. "Let's practise first. In here, the water is pretend."

"Step one: blowing bubbles. Breathe in through your mouth… then blow out slowly, like blowing out a candle. Whoooo!"

Abi filled a basin with real water. Mo blew… blub-blub-blub! Bubbles popped up. Mo giggled.

"When you're blowing out, water can't get up your nose," said Abi. "Your nose has a guard: your own breath."

"Step two: kicking like a frog!" Mo lay on his tummy on the rug and kicked. Thump-thump-thump!

"Step three: wearing a float and trusting." Ais put the duck float around Mo's waist. "You can do it, Mo!"

That night Mo was still a little scared. Abi taught him a dua. "When something feels hard, ask Allah to make it easy."

> *"Allahumma la sahla illa ma ja'altahu sahla, wa anta taj'alul hazna idha shi'ta sahla." — O Allah, nothing is easy except what You make easy, and You make the hard easy if You will. (Ibn Hibban 2427)*

The next day, at the swimming pool, the water felt cold. Mo's legs trembled. He held Abi's hand tight. "Bismillah…"

Mo remembered step one. Breathe in… face in… blub-blub-blub! His first bubbles in the pool!

Then step two — kick like a frog! Thump-thump-thump! The duck float carried him forward a little… a little… all the way to Abi!

"MASYA ALLAH, MO!" Ais cheered from the side. Mo laughed so hard the water splashed everywhere.

"Abi, the scared feeling gets smaller when you try a little at a time," said Mo, shivering in his towel. "And when Allah makes it easy," added Abi.

That night Mo fell asleep smiling. In his dream, he swam with the fish — blub, blub, blub.
