> *"Bismillahi tawakkaltu 'alallah, laa hawla wa laa quwwata illaa billaah." — In Allah's name, I trust in Allah. (Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi)*

At the entrance, Abi knelt down. "Remember the three magic steps if we get separated? One: STOP. Two: find a SAFE HELPER. Three: say ABI'S NUMBER."

"And Abi's number has a song!" They sang: "Oh-eight-one-two, three-four-five-six, seven-eight-nine — that's Abi's number!"

The supermarket was huge. The shelves were tall and colourful. Baby Mo proudly pushed a little trolley.

Then Baby Mo saw a red balloon floating in the next aisle. "A balloon!" He ran after it… pitter-patter…

The balloon vanished behind a shelf. Baby Mo turned round. Tall shelves. Strangers. Where was Abi? Where was Ais?

His heart pounded. He wanted to run anywhere and shout. His eyes started to fill with tears…

Then he remembered Abi's voice: "One: STOP." Baby Mo stopped. Breathe in… breathe out. "Abi will come back to look for me here."

"Two: find a SAFE HELPER." Not just anyone — a cashier or security guard in uniform, or a mum who's with her own children.

A man he didn't know said, "Come outside with me, we'll find your dad in the car park." Baby Mo shook his head hard. "No. I'm waiting inside!"

He walked to the cashier's desk, to the lady in the blue uniform. "Excuse me, I got separated from Abi." She smiled and crouched down. "You were so clever to come here."

"Three: ABI'S NUMBER!" Baby Mo sang: "Oh-eight-one-two, three-four-five-six, seven-eight-nine!" The cashier made the call.

Soon he heard the voice he knew best: "MO!" Abi came running with Baby Ais and hugged him very tight.

"I was so scared," Abi whispered. "But you were brilliant: you stopped, found a safe helper and remembered my number. Thank you for using the three magic steps."

They thanked the cashier. Baby Ais immediately asked to learn the Abi's-number song too.
