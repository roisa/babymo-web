3b:T601,On Saturday Mo and Ais played all morning. Blocks, dolls, cars, books — everything came out.

You couldn't see the playroom floor any more. It was all toys!

Abi came in carrying tea. One step, two steps… CRUNCH! “OUCH! What's this?” He'd stepped on a block.

Mo and Ais looked at each other. “Sorry, Abi…” Mo scratched his head. “We forgot to tidy up.”

Abi sat on the rug. “Did you know? Toys have homes too. If they don't go home, they get lost.”

Abi stuck pictures on the boxes: one for blocks, one for dolls, one for cars, one for books.

“Now,” said Abi, turning the sand timer, “whose toys can get home fastest?”

Mo picked up blocks as fast as lightning. Clatter, clatter, clatter — the blocks went home.

Ais tucked the dolls into their box. “Night night, Teddy. Night night, Bunny.”

The books lined up neatly on the shelf, from tallest to shortest.

The sand ran out just as the last car parked in its garage. “DONE!” they both shouted.

The playroom was big again. The floor was clear. Abi could walk without saying “ouch”.

“Masha Allah, so tidy,” said Abi. “Allah is beautiful and loves beauty. A tidy home is beautiful.”
