Who Falls Asleep First?

👨 With AbiBook 7 of 11← PreviousNext →

Abi's on bedtime duty tonight. But the story is too long… and the first one to fall asleep is Abi!

That night Umi was staying at Grandma's. "Tonight I'll put you to bed," said Abi bravely.

Teeth brushed — done. Pyjamas on — done. Wee first — done. "Now into bed!"

"Story first, Abi! A looong one!" begged Baby Mo. Baby Ais nodded and nodded.

Abi opened the book. "In a forest there lived a giraffe whose neck was very… very… long…"

"…the giraffe walked… and walked…" Abi yawned. "Hooaaam… and walked…"

Abi's head nodded and nodded. The book tilted. "Abi?" Silence. Then: "Snorrre… pssst… snorrre…"

The twins looked at each other… then giggled behind their hands. "Abi fell asleep first!"

"Now we put Abi to bed," whispered Baby Ais. "Ant mode!"

Baby Mo slid a pillow under Abi's head. Baby Ais pulled the blanket right up to his chin.

Baby Mo put his teddy next to Abi. "So Abi isn't scared of the dark."

Then they switched off the light. Click. Only the moon peeked in through the window.

"We haven't said our du'a," said Baby Ais. They lay down and prayed softly: "Bismika Allaahumma ahyaa wa amuut."

"In Your name, O Allah, I live and I die." (Bukhari)

"And one for Abi too," added Baby Mo. "O Allah, let Abi dream the giraffe gets there."

At midnight Abi woke up. There was a pillow, a blanket, a teddy… and two children fast asleep.

Abi laughed without a sound. "So who put who to bed?" He kissed them both on the forehead.

The next morning Abi said, "Tonight, I'll tell a short story!" The twins laughed. Who usually falls asleep first at your house? 🦒

A card for parents

A Warm Bedtime Routine

  1. 1Keep the same order every night: teeth, wudu, a story, du'a, lights down.
  2. 2One short story is enough, even when you're tired.
  3. 3End with the bedtime du'a and one thing you're grateful for today.
