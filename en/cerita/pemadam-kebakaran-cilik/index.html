The Little Firefighters

🧸 Playroom AdventuresBook 4 of 16← PreviousNext →

Nee-naw nee-naw! Abi is the chief and the twins are firefighters. Mo wants to rescue Mr Bear all by himself — until he slips off the ladder.

Nee-naw nee-naw! Abi had a day off, and the playroom turned into a fire station.

"Chief Abi reporting! Firefighter Mo and Firefighter Ais, ready for duty?" "READY, CHIEF!"

"Emergency! Mr Bear is stuck at the top of the tower!" High on the block tower, Mr Bear waited to be rescued.

"I'll do it myself!" shouted Mo. He ran to the ladder without waiting for anyone.

One rung, two rungs… wobble… CRASH! Mo slipped and landed on his bottom. His knee hurt.

Abi knelt and checked Mo's knee. "You're okay, Firefighter Mo. But do you know what? Real firefighters never work alone."

"One holds the ladder, one sprays the water, one carries the person to safety. When we work together, everyone is safe."

This time Ais held the ladder tight. Mo climbed slowly. One… two… three… "Mr Bear, I'm coming!"

Mo carried Mr Bear down, and Abi sprayed "water" from the toy hose. Pssshhh! The fire was out!

"Mission complete!" They did a three-way high five. Mr Bear was safe, and nobody else got hurt.

Then Chief Abi sat down seriously. "Now a real firefighter lesson. Fire is hot and dangerous. Matches, stoves and candles are not toys. If you see fire, don't go near it — call a grown-up."

"If there's smoke, crawl low underneath it and get out together," said Abi. The twins practised crawling like turtles. It was so funny!

"Abi, firefighters are heroes, aren't they?" asked Ais. "Yes. Anyone who helps others is a hero. And Allah always helps those who help others."

"Allah helps His servant as long as the servant helps his brother." (Muslim 2699)

That afternoon, Umi came home with lots of shopping. Mo and Ais looked at each other. "Emergency! Umi needs help!" They ran to help.

Mo carried the vegetable bag and Ais carried the bread. "Thank you, my little firefighters," said Umi. Helping felt so warm inside.

Who can you help today? And remember: fire is not a toy! 🚒

A card for parents

Fire Rules for Little Ones

  1. 1Matches, stoves, candles and sockets are not toys. Keep lighters well out of reach.
  2. 2Practise together: if there's smoke, crawl low, get outside and call a grown-up. Practice through play sticks.
  3. 3Learn your local emergency number together (113 for the fire service in Indonesia) and a family meeting spot outside.
