Inside the glass was a big rock. And behind the rock… two round eyes were peeking out!

It was an octopus! Its colour kept changing: grey like the rock, then purple, then grey again. The octopus was feeling shy.

The guide asked, "Who knows how many arms an octopus has?" Mo knew the answer: eight! But his voice got stuck in his throat.

Umi knelt down and whispered, "It's okay to feel shy, love. Look, the octopus needs time too."

Did you know? An octopus has three hearts and blue blood! When it's scared, it can squeeze into a gap as small as a coin.

Mo sat quietly in front of the glass. He didn't tap on it. He didn't shout. He just waited, very patiently.

Slowly… slowly… the octopus came out from behind the rock. One arm, two arms… all eight arms!

Then the octopus pressed an arm against the glass, right in front of Mo, as if it were waving: "Hi!"

"Umi, the octopus came out because we didn't force it," whispered Mo. His heart felt warm.

Umi taught him the du'a of Prophet Musa for when he felt nervous about speaking. Mo repeated it slowly.
