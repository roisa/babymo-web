32:T91e,That night, torches were glowing in the street. "What's happening, Kak? Is it Eid again?" asked Mo. Kak Adam laughed. "No! Tomorrow is 1 Muharram — the Islamic New Year!"

"Another New Year? Isn't New Year in January?" Mo scratched his head, very confused.

"There are two calendars," Abi explained. "The Gregorian calendar follows the sun. The Hijri calendar follows the moon — from one new crescent to the next."

"There are twelve months," said Umi. Then she sang: "Muharram, Safar, Rabi' al-Awwal, Rabi' al-Akhir, Jumada al-Ula, Jumada al-Akhirah…"

> *"Indeed, the number of months with Allah is twelve, in the decree of Allah from the day He created the heavens and the earth…" (Qur'an, At-Tawbah 9:36)*

"…Rajab, Sha'ban, Ramadan, Shawwal, Dhul-Qa'dah, Dhul-Hijjah!" The twins sang and danced along until they knew it by heart.

"Why is it called Hijri?" asked Ais. Abi told them about the Hijrah: the Prophet ﷺ and his companions moved from Makkah to Madinah, so they could worship in safety.

"On the way, the Prophet ﷺ and his friend Abu Bakr hid in a cave. Abu Bakr was worried. But the Prophet ﷺ said: Do not be sad, Allah is with us."

> *"…when he said to his companion: Do not grieve, indeed Allah is with us." (Qur'an, At-Tawbah 9:40)*

"Allah is with us…" whispered Mo. "So when I'm scared of the dark, Allah is with me too?" "Always," said Abi with a smile.

"The Islamic calendar is counted from the Hijrah," said Abi. "The caliph Umar ibn al-Khattab decided it." Mo nodded like a little professor.

"Hijrah means moving," said Kak Adam. "We can make a hijrah too — moving from a bad habit to a good one!"

Then they joined the torch parade around the neighbourhood. Little flames danced all along the road. "Happy Islamic New Year!"

Back home, everyone wrote a "little hijrah". Mo wrote: "No more grabbing toys." Kak Adam wrote: "Don't get cross when I lose at tennis."

Ais wrote: "Make my own bed every morning." They stuck them on the fridge. "One year, one little hijrah. Bismillah!"

On the morning of 1 Muharram, Ais was playing with blocks. Mo really wanted to grab them… but he remembered his hijrah. "You go first, Ais." Ais gave a huge smile.

Abi gave a thumbs up. "Happy Hijri New Year! Your first hijrah worked, Mo!"
