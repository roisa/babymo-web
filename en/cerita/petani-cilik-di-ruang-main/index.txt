34:T948,Dinner was ready. But Mo only ate three spoonfuls. "I'm full," he said, pushing his plate away. There was still lots of rice and carrot left.

Umi didn't get cross. She only asked, "Mo, do you know where this rice comes from?" "The rice cooker!" Mo said confidently. Ais giggled.

The next morning, Abi opened the door to the farm playroom. There was a red barn, green fields and a windmill. "Today we're farmers!"

First job: planting rice. Abi gave them a handful of seeds. "One seed, one hole. Gently now."

"Then we wait," said Abi. "Watering it, guarding it from birds and bugs, for four months." "FOUR MONTHS?!" cried Mo.

Second job: feeding the cow. Mooooo! A cow needs grass and water every day before she can give milk.

Third job: collecting eggs. Ais found one… two… three! "The hens have to eat and drink first before they can lay," said Abi.

Fourth job: pulling up carrots. Mo pulled with all his might. Heave… heave… PLOP! He landed on his bottom holding a huge carrot.

Mo flopped down, worn out. "Abi… being a farmer is so tiring." "Yes," said Abi. "And every bit of food on our plates passed through tired hands like that."

"Farmers plant, herders feed the animals, drivers deliver, sellers sell, Umi cooks… But who makes the rice grow?" "Allah!" answered the twins.

> *"Then let mankind look at their food: how We pour down water, then split the earth, and cause grain to grow in it." (Qur'an, 'Abasa 80:24–27)*

Mo remembered his plate from last night. The rice he didn't eat… the carrots he pushed away… He went quiet.

That evening, Umi served rice, omelette and carrot soup. This time Mo took a small portion first. "Bismillah."

Spoonful by spoonful… all gone! He even picked up a grain of rice that fell. "It came a long way," he said seriously.

> *"If any of you drops a morsel, let them pick it up, wipe off any dirt and eat it, and not leave it for Shaytan." (Muslim 2033)*

Umi smiled wide. "Masya Allah. If your tummy is small, take a little first. If you're still hungry, you can have more." Mo nodded.

After the meal, the twins said the dua together. Thank you, Allah. Thank you, farmers.

> *"Alhamdulillahilladhi at'amana wa saqana wa ja'alana muslimin." — All praise is for Allah who gave us food and drink and made us Muslims. (Abu Dawud 3850)*
