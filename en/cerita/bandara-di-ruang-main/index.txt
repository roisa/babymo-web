Kak Adam had an idea. "The Playroom Airport is open! Passenger Mo and Passenger Ais, please come in!"

First, queue at the counter. Ais handed over her paper ticket. "Destination: Grandma's House. Seat number one!"

Second, the bag check. Mo pushed his backpack through the cardboard tunnel. Beep-beep! "All clear! Teddy and biscuits," said Officer Kak Adam.

Third, sit down and fasten your seatbelt. Click! Kak Adam put on a pilot's cap. "Good afternoon, passengers. This is Captain Adam speaking."

"Before we fly, we say the travelling dua," said Captain Adam. Everyone raised their hands.

> *"Subhanalladhi sakhkhara lana hadha wa ma kunna lahu muqrinin, wa inna ila rabbina lamunqalibun." — Glory be to Him who has subjected this to us, for we could never have done it ourselves, and to our Lord we shall surely return. (Qur'an, Az-Zukhruf 43:13–14)*

"Prepare for take-off!" Kak Adam made the engine sound: "NYEEEEEEOW!" Mo covered his ears… but then peeked.

"It's loud because the engines are working hard to lift us," said Kak Adam. "That's the sound of a healthy plane!"

Mo lowered his hands. Then he spread his arms like wings. "NYEEEOW! I'm a plane too!"

They "flew" over pillow clouds, cardboard mountains and a blue-rug sea. "There's Grandma's house down there!" shouted Ais.

"We're about to land. Seatbelts on!" Bump… bump… A smooth landing. Everyone clapped.

A week later, at the real airport, everything felt familiar: queue, bag check, sit down, seatbelt. Click!

The engines roared NYEEEOW. Mo held Umi's hand and said the dua all by himself, softly and all the way through. "Subhanalladhi sakhkhara lana…"

Outside the window, white clouds drifted by like cotton. "Umi, I'm not scared any more. I'm happy!"

At Grandma's house, Grandma was waiting at the door with open arms. "My grandchildren are here! Alhamdulillah!"
