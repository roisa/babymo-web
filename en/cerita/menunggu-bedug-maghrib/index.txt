36:T8c3,The fifteenth day of Ramadan. Look at the calendar — fifteen stars already! Today Mo and Ais want to fast all the way to sunset.

In the afternoon, Umi called the twins into the kitchen. "Let's make banana kolak. Half for us, half to share."

Mo sliced the bananas. Ais poured in the coconut milk. The smell of palm sugar filled the kitchen. Mmm… so good!

Mo's tummy sang again: grrrumble. "Umi, can we eat the kolak now?" Umi laughed. "Not yet, love. Wait for sunset. Patience has its reward."

Kak Adam carried a bowl of kolak for Pak Rahmat, the neighbour who lives on his own. Mo walked beside him.

Knock, knock, knock. "Assalamu'alaikum, Pak Rahmat! Here's some kolak for iftar," said Mo. Pak Rahmat smiled so much his eyes went shiny. "Thank you, son."

On the way home, Mo remembered what Abi had said: whoever gives food to a fasting person gets a reward like that person's fast.

> *"Whoever gives food to a fasting person to break their fast will have a reward like theirs, without their reward being reduced at all." (Tirmidhi 807, hasan sahih)*

Then Ais and Umi took the rest of the kolak to the mosque. On the porch stood a long table full of iftar treats for anyone passing by.

Five o'clock. Time felt so slow. Mo counted the floor tiles. Ais counted the clouds. "One… two… three…"

Half past five. Dates, water, kolak and fried snacks were ready on the table. Nobody touched them. Everyone waited.

"Not long now," whispered Abi. "Waiting for iftar is a good time for du'a. Ask Allah for anything."

Then… DUM! DUM! DUM! "Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar…" The sunset call to prayer! "Alhamdulillah!" cried Mo and Ais together.

They broke their fast with dates and water, like the Prophet ﷺ. Then they said the du'a together: "Dhahaba al-zama'u wabtallat al-'uruqu wa thabata al-ajru in sha Allah."

> *"The thirst is gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is certain, if Allah wills." (Abu Dawud 2357)*

That water was the freshest in the whole world. The dates were the sweetest ever. "Umi… fasting makes everything taste amazing!"

That night, Mo stuck a star on the calendar. Then he whispered, "O Allah, I hope Pak Rahmat had a happy iftar too."
