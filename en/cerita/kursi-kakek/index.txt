2f:T938,At Nenek's house there was a soft brown armchair. It was Kakek's chair. That's where he always sat, doing dhikr with his green prayer beads.

Kakek loved to sit Mo on his lap and tell stories about his village: a lazy water buffalo, a clear river and kites flying high.

One morning the phone rang. Abi listened for a long time, then slowly sat down. "Innaa lillaahi wa innaa ilaihi raaji'uun."

Abi called the children over. "Kakek died this morning," he said gently. "His body stopped working, and Allah has called him home."

"When is Kakek coming back?" asked Mo. Abi hugged him. "Kakek won't come back to this world, love. But he isn't in pain any more."

Mo saw that Abi's eyes were wet. "Abi, are you crying?" "Yes. I'm sad because I miss him. The Prophet ﷺ cried too when his little son died. It's okay to cry."

> *"The eyes shed tears and the heart grieves, but we say only what pleases our Lord." (Bukhari)*

At Nenek's house, many people came to pray for Kakek. Ais cried in Umi's lap. Umi let her cry until she felt better.

Kakek's chair was empty. His peci and green prayer beads were still there. Mo gently touched the beads, one, two…

"Abi," Mo asked, "can Kakek still get presents from us?"

Abi smiled. "Yes. The Prophet ﷺ said three things keep flowing to a person after they die. One of them is the du'a of a good child."

> *"When a person dies, their deeds end except three: ongoing charity, beneficial knowledge, or a righteous child who prays for them." (Muslim)*

"I'm Kakek's grandson," said Mo. "Can I pray for him?" "Of course, love. Your du'a is the most beautiful present Kakek could get."

Mo raised both his hands. "O Allah, forgive Kakek, have mercy on him, make his grave spacious, and bring us together again in Your Paradise."

> *"Allaahummaghfir lahu warhamhu wa 'aafihi wa'fu 'anhu." — O Allah, forgive him, have mercy on him, keep him safe and pardon him. (Muslim)*

Kak Adam put his savings into the charity box. "In Kakek's name," he said, "so the reward keeps flowing to him."

That night, Mo and Ais drew Kakek's stories: the lazy buffalo, the clear river and Kakek smiling. They missed him… but they smiled too.

Umi hugged Mo before bed. "Missing someone is a sign of love. Whenever you miss Kakek, send him a du'a."
