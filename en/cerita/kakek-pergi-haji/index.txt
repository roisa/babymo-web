"Pray for me," Grandpa had said before leaving. The twins raised their hands and prayed for Grandpa to be healthy and safe.

Abi spread out a map. "Let's follow Grandpa's Hajj, step by step!" Ais was ready to be an explorer.

Step one: ihram. Grandpa wore two plain white cloths and called out the talbiyah: "Labbayk Allahumma labbayk!" It means, "Here I am, O Allah, answering Your call!"

> *The Prophet's talbiyah ﷺ: "Labbayk Allahumma labbayk, labbayka la sharika laka labbayk, innal-hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk, la sharika lak." (Bukhari 1549)*

That night Grandpa video-called. On the screen was the Ka'bah, surrounded by a sea of people in white. "Masya Allah!" cried Mo.

"Grandpa is doing tawaf," said Abi. "Walking around the Ka'bah seven times, remembering Allah and making du'a."

The next day, the twins played little pilgrims like at kindergarten: walking round the rug seven times. "One… two… three…" On lap seven, Mo was dizzy! Hee hee.

Then sa'i: walking back and forth between the hills of Safa and Marwah seven times. Abi told them how Hajar ran to and fro looking for water for her baby, Ismail.

"Then Allah made water gush from the ground — zamzam! It's still flowing today," said Abi. "Wow!" said Mo and Ais together.

On 9 Dhul-Hijjah, Grandpa stood at the plain of Arafah — praying from midday until sunset. The most important day of Hajj.

"We can join in from home too," said Umi. "By fasting on the day of Arafah. The reward is huge!" Ais wanted to try straight away.

> *"Fasting the day of Arafah, I hope Allah will wipe out the sins of the year before it and the year after it." (Muslim 1162)*

After that, Grandpa threw pebbles at the pillars, a sign that we resist Shaytan's whispers. Mo pretended to throw. "Hup! Go away, Shaytan!"

Almost a month later, Grandpa came home! His face was glowing. He brought dates and zamzam water. "Grandpaaa!" Mo jumped for joy.

"Grandpa, what was Hajj like?" asked Ais. Grandpa said, "Millions of people from all over the world, dressed the same, praying to the same Allah. It felt like one big family."

Abi smiled. "An accepted Hajj is rewarded with nothing less than Paradise." Mo held Ais's hand. "When we're grown up, let's go on Hajj together!"
