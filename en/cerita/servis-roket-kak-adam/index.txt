"Easy!" said Kak Adam. He tossed the ball… and it dropped straight onto his head. BONK!

The second toss went too far back. The third went off to the side — splosh! — straight into a puddle.

Baby Mo laughed so hard he rolled over. "Your ball wants a bath, Kak!"

Kak Adam laughed too, then sighed. "Serving is hard, Abi. There's so much to remember."

"Then let's break it into little pieces," said Abi. "Today we only practise the toss. No racket."

"Imagine a shelf in the sky," said Abi. "Lift the ball gently and place it on that shelf. Don't throw it — place it."

Up… pause… down. The ball dropped back into his hand. Up… pause… down. Again and again.

Baby Mo practised too, with his red ball. It fell on his own nose. "Ouch! But it's fun!"

The next day, the racket could join in. Abi taught the rhythm: "Toss… wait… HIT!"

First serve: into the net. Second: out. Third: the net again. Kak Adam bit his lip.

Abi asked him to stop for a moment and pray: "O Allah, nothing is easy except what You make easy."

"Bismillah." Kak Adam placed the ball on the sky shelf. Waited. Then… CRACK!

The ball shot off like a rocket, over the net, and bounced right in the service box!

"ROCKET SERVE!" shouted Baby Mo and Baby Ais. They jumped and spun around.
