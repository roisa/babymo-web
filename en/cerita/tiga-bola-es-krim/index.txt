"Three scoops is a lot for a little tummy," said Umi. "Pleeease, Umi!" Mo begged with his hands together.

Umi smiled. "All right. But eat it slowly." Mo got a very tall ice-cream tower. Chocolate. Strawberry. Vanilla.

Mo forgot to sit down. Mo forgot Bismillah. He took big, big bites straight away. Yum! Yum! YUM!

Suddenly — OUCHHH! Mo's head felt freezing cold, as if there was snow inside it. "Ow, ow, my head's frozen!"

The third scoop melted slowly in the cup. Drip… drip… drip. Such a waste, but Mo had no room left.

Umi sat beside Mo. "The Prophet ﷺ taught that the tummy is like a house with three rooms: a third for food, a third for drink and a third for breathing."

> *"No human fills a vessel worse than his stomach. A few bites are enough to keep his back straight. If he must, then a third for food, a third for drink and a third for breath." (Tirmidhi 2380; hasan sahih)*

"So my breathing room got filled with ice cream," said Mo. Umi laughed. "Yes! That's why it felt so tight."

Ais ate her ice cream very slowly. One spoonful… smile. One spoonful… smile. "How come Ais's ice cream tastes better?" asked Mo.

"Because I sat down, said Bismillah and enjoyed every spoonful," said Ais proudly. "The taste lasts longer!"

The next week, Mo ordered ONE scoop of chocolate. He sat down. "Bismillah." One spoonful… smile. One spoonful… smile.

"Umi! One scoop eaten slowly is yummier than three scoops eaten fast!" And his breathing room stayed nice and roomy. Alhamdulillah.
