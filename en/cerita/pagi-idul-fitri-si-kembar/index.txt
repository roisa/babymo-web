37:T938,"Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar…" The takbir from the mosque woke Mo very early. He leapt out of bed. "It's Eid today!"

Umi had warm water ready. "Bath first. We start Eid clean and fresh."

Mo put on his best clothes. Ais wore a sparkly new headscarf. They looked at each other. "Masya Allah, Ais, you look lovely!" "You look smart too, Mo!"

Before they left, Abi handed out dates. "The Prophet ﷺ ate dates before the Eid al-Fitr prayer. An odd number of them."

> *"The Prophet ﷺ would not go out on the day of Eid al-Fitr until he had eaten some dates… and he ate an odd number." (Bukhari 953)*

Mo counted his dates. "One… two… three! Three is odd!" Then he wanted more. "Four? Oops, four is even. Five, then!" Hee hee.

All the way to the prayer field, everyone called out the takbir: "Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, la ilaha illallah…" Voices answered from every house.

The field was already full. Prayer mats lay in rows, colourful as a rainbow. Mo and Ais took off their sandals and left them at the edge.

"Abi, why is there no call to prayer?" asked Ais. "The Eid prayer has no adhan or iqamah," said Abi. "It starts straight away with takbir."

> *Jabir ibn Abdullah said: "I attended Eid with the Prophet ﷺ. He began with the prayer before the sermon, with no adhan and no iqamah." (Muslim 885)*

The prayer began. In the first rakaat there were lots of takbirs — seven! Mo raised his hands each time… then lost count. Hee hee.

After the prayer, everyone listened to the sermon. Mo sat nicely, even though his feet itched to run. Ais sat calmly beside him.

Then everyone shook hands and prayed for each other. "Taqabbalallahu minna wa minkum — may Allah accept from us and you!" Smiles everywhere.

When it was time to go, Mo looked for his sandals… Huh? One green, one yellow! "Whose sandal is this?"

A boy ran over, laughing. "That's my sandal! We got them mixed up!" They swapped sandals and laughed together.

Abi took them home a different way. "The Prophet ﷺ came back from the Eid prayer by another road. That way we can greet more people."

> *"On the day of Eid, the Prophet ﷺ would take a different route (going and returning)." (Bukhari 986)*

On the new road they met neighbours, school friends, even Pak Rahmat! "Happy Eid!" Mo waved until his arm ached.
