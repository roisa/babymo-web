The Little Bee and the Honey from Allah

Bzzz! Mo wants to swat the bee away. But bees dance, work as a team and make honey — they even have their own surah in the Qur'an!

Bzzz! A bee flew close to Mo. "Go away! Go away!" Mo flapped his hands, frightened.

"Stay calm, Mo," Umi said softly. "Freeze like a statue. Bees don't sting unless they feel threatened."

Mo stayed still… very still… The bee buzzed around him, then flew off to a flower. Phew!

"Want to see a real bee house?" asked Umi. That afternoon, Umi took the twins to Pak Darto's bee farm.

There were rows of wooden boxes. Inside each box, thousands of bees were working together. Bzzz-bzzz-bzzz!

"Bees build their homes from wax in perfect six-sided shapes," said Pak Darto. Ais counted the sides: one, two… six!

Did you know? When a bee finds flowers, it flies home and does a dance. The dance tells its friends where the flowers are!

As they drink nectar, bees carry pollen from flower to flower. That's how flowers turn into fruit — apples, oranges and watermelons!

"In its whole life, one bee makes only a tiny drop of honey — less than a teaspoon," said Pak Darto. "But together, they fill a whole jar!"

Umi smiled. "There's a surah in the Qur'an called An-Nahl, which means The Bee. Allah Himself taught the bees how to build their homes."

"And your Lord inspired the bee: 'Make homes in the mountains and the trees…' From their bellies comes a drink of varying colours, in which there is healing for people." (Qur'an, An-Nahl 16:68–69)

"Bees have their own surah?" Mo's eyes went wide. "Wow, bees must be really special!"

Pak Darto gave them each a spoonful of honey. Sweet, fragrant and warm in their throats. "Alhamdulillah," said Mo with a big smile.

Mo remembered the bee from that morning. "Sorry, little bee. I thought you were mean. You're actually a hard worker."

At home, Mo and Ais planted flowers in the garden. "So the bees have a restaurant!" said Ais.

A few days later, a bee visited their flowers. This time Mo didn't swat. He just sat calmly and whispered, "Enjoy your meal, little bee."

If a bee comes close to you, what should you do? Freeze like a statue! 🐝

A card for parents

Being Brave Around Bees and Bugs

  1. 1Teach "freeze like a statue", then walk away slowly. Swatting makes bees feel threatened.
  2. 2If stung: remove the sting and use a cold compress. Get help quickly if the face swells or your child struggles to breathe.
  3. 3Never give honey to babies under 1 year old. For older children, honey is a lovely way to talk about Allah's signs.
