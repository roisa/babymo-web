The first guests arrived: Abi and Umi! "We'd like… the tastiest soup in the world," said Abi.

Mo ran to the real kitchen. "I want to use the big knife and the stove, like a real chef!"

Umi hurried after him. "Hold on, Chef Mo. Sharp knives and a hot stove are grown-up jobs. Little chefs have their own important jobs."

"Little chefs' jobs: washing vegetables, stirring the batter, laying the plates, and… tasting!" Mo and Ais laughed.

Mo washed the tomatoes and carrots in a bowl. Ais stirred the batter. Stir… stir… stir!

Umi chopped the vegetables and lit the stove. The twins watched from the safe line — three steps from the stove.

Abi picked up the spoon with his left hand… then stopped. "Hmm, chefs, I've forgotten something. What is it?"

"BISMILLAH!" called Ais. "And use your right hand!" added Mo. Abi laughed. "Clever! These chefs know their table manners too."

Umi told them about a little boy called Umar whose hand wandered all over the dish while he ate. The Prophet ﷺ advised him gently.

"Delicious!" said Abi and Umi together. "Alhamdulillah," said Mo proudly. "Umi's cooking, our helping!"

The last guest arrived: Kak Adam, hungry after tennis practice. "Any soup left?" Mo gave him his own bowl — the chef's portion!

The restaurant closed. The twins washed the plastic plates and wiped the table. Great chefs always tidy their kitchen.
