2e:T737,It was a quiet afternoon. Mo was building a block tower, Ais was drawing, and Umi was watering the plants by the window.

Suddenly the floor began to shake! The lamp swung, the glasses rattled, the picture frame tilted. RUMBLE… "Umi!" shouted Mo.

Umi called out in a calm voice: "Earthquake! Drop, cover, hold on!"

The twins dropped down under the sturdy table, covered their heads with their hands, and held on to the table leg.

Umi took cover next to them, away from the window and the cupboard. Softly she whispered, "Hasbunallah wa ni'mal wakiil."

> *"Hasbunallaahu wa ni'mal wakiil." — Allah is enough for us, and He is the best Protector. (Qur'an, Ali 'Imran 3:173)*

Then the shaking stopped. Silence. Mo was still trembling, and tears began to fall.

Umi held Mo and Ais tight. "You were wonderful. You did everything exactly right."

Once the shaking had stopped, they left the house calmly — walking, not running — towards the open field.

At the meeting point, the neighbours had gathered. Abi came from the mosque and went straight to the twins. "Alhamdulillah, you're safe."

"The earth is like a giant layer cake that sometimes shifts a little," Abi explained. "Indonesia has lots of earthquakes, so we always need to be ready."

"What if it comes again?" Mo asked quietly. "We know what to do now," said Abi. "And Allah is always with us."

That evening they packed a go-bag: a torch, water, medicine, bread and a whistle. "The whistle is for calling for help," said Abi.

Abi made it into a practice game. Whenever Abi said "Earthquake!", everyone dived under the table as fast as lightning!

Ais was the fastest! "Drop, cover, hold on!" she cheered proudly.

Before bed, they made du'a for Allah to protect everyone whose homes had been hit by earthquakes.
