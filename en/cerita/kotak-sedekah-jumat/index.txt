Baby Mo had one coin from his piggy bank. He looked at the coin… then at the sweet shop down the road.

"If I put my coin in the box, I can't buy sweets," he thought. His face scrunched up.

Kak Adam sat down beside him. "Did you know, Mo? Charity doesn't have to be money."
