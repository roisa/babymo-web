Crack… crack… The eggs began to split. A tiny head popped out of the sand! "A baby turtle!" shouted Ais.

Kak Adam read the sign on the beach: baby turtles find the sea by looking for moonlight on the water. "So tonight, no torches or phone lights!"

Mo wanted to pick one up. But Abi gently shook his head. "Let them walk on their own. Those first steps on the sand make them strong, and they'll remember this beach."

The moon came up. The baby turtles waddled towards the sea. So small, but they never stopped.

But one baby turtle got stuck behind a plastic cup lying on the sand. It turned round and round, confused.

Kak Adam gently picked the litter up. The way was clear! The little turtle crawled on towards the waves.

In the sea, the turtle will grow big. Turtles love to eat jellyfish. But a plastic bag in the water… looks just like a jellyfish.

"If a turtle eats plastic, its tummy can get very sick," Abi said quietly. Mo held Abi's hand tight.

"Allah has entrusted the land and the sea to us," said Abi. "Our job is to look after them, not to spoil them."
