“Just like your eyes. They need a rest too, and to look at faraway things.”

> *The Prophet ﷺ said, “There are two blessings that many people waste: health and free time.” (Bukhari 6412)*

Then Ais came in with a big cardboard box. “Mo! Look! We can make a rocket out of this!”

Mo wiped his tears. A rocket? From a box? He crept closer.

They cut out windows, drew stars and stuck on a tail of red paper flames.

“Three… two… one… BLAST OFF!” Mo and Ais sat in the rocket, flying to the moon.

They played until the afternoon. Nobody remembered the tablet at all.

At the Maghrib adhan Umi said, “In our home, during the adhan and at meals, all screens rest. Including Umi's and Abi's phones.”

The next day, before watching, Mo turned the sand timer himself. “Three videos, then the tablet has a rest.”

When the sand ran out, Mo switched off the tablet and said, “Have a nice rest! Ais, let's fly to the moon again!”
