2f:T818,It was a very hot afternoon. Mo had just finished playing ball in the garden. Sweat dripped down his face. He flopped onto the grass. "So thirsty…"

Mo ran to the kitchen, grabbed a glass, filled it right up and… gulp, gulp, gulp! "Aaah, so refreshing!"

Ais walked into the kitchen. Her eyes went as round as balls. "MO! You're fasting!"

Mo clapped his hand over his mouth. "Astaghfirullah! I forgot!" His eyes filled with tears. "My fast is broken… I've failed…"

Umi came and sat beside Mo. "It's all right, love. Did you drink on purpose, or because you forgot?" "I forgot, Umi… honestly."

Umi smiled. "If you forgot, your fast isn't broken. It was Allah who gave you that drink. Carry on with your fast."

> *"Whoever forgets while fasting and eats or drinks, let them complete their fast, for it was Allah who fed them and gave them drink." (Bukhari 1933, Muslim 1155)*

"Allah gave me a drink?" Mo stared at his empty glass for a long time. "Masya Allah… Allah is so kind."

"But remember," said Umi, "stop the moment you realise. If you remember and still keep drinking, then it's broken." Mo nodded very seriously.

Ais put up her hand. "Umi, if I see someone forget, should I tell them?" "Yes, remind them," said Umi. "But gently. Not by shouting MO!"

Ais giggled, embarrassed. "Sorry, Mo. I was surprised." Mo laughed too. "That's okay. Thanks for reminding me."

Later that afternoon, Kak Adam came home from tennis, tired and thirsty. He picked up his water bottle and twisted off the cap…

The twins looked at each other. Then they whispered together, ever so softly: "Kak Adam… you're fasting, you know."

Kak Adam stopped. He scratched his head and laughed. "Wow, thank you, you two! I nearly forgot. You're the best reminders!"

At iftar, Abi laughed at the story. "Today you learned three things: honesty, reminding gently, and that Allah is the Most Generous."

That night, Mo prayed before bed. "Thank you, O Allah, for giving me a drink when I forgot."
