2e:T705,“I need the bathroom!” said Baby Mo. “But this time… BY MYSELF. Like Kak Adam!”

Abi smiled. “Okay. But remember the steps. Shall I help you remember?” Mo nodded.

Step one: stop at the door and say the du'a. “Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal khubuthi wal khaba'ith.”

> *Anas ibn Malik said that when the Prophet ﷺ entered the toilet, he would say, “Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal khubuthi wal khaba'ith.” (Bukhari 142, Muslim 375)*

It means: O Allah, I seek refuge in You from all the bad things. Step two: go in with the left foot.

Mo shut the door. Inside, he did everything by himself. Then… oops, he nearly forgot to flush!

Whoosh! Mo flushed until it was clean. “Allah loves clean,” he said, copying Abi.

> *The Prophet ﷺ said, “Purity is half of faith.” (Muslim 223)*

Next step: wash hands with soap. Rub palms, backs of hands, between fingers — counting to twenty.

But there was too much foam! Water splashed on the mirror, the floor and Mo's nose. “Hee hee!”

Mo took a cloth and dried the floor himself. Being independent means tidying up your own splashes too.

Last step: step out with the right foot, then say, “Ghufranak.” It means: I ask for Your forgiveness, O Allah.

“Abi! I did it! I remembered everything myself!” Mo bounced so hard his sandal flew off.

Abi laughed. “Masha Allah, you're growing up.” Mo puffed out his chest. He felt so tall.

That night Mo woke up needing the bathroom. He remembered: stop, du'a, left foot…

…flush, wash hands, right foot, “Ghufranak.” Then back to bed without waking anyone.

The next morning Mo drew the steps to stick on the door — so Ais could learn too.
