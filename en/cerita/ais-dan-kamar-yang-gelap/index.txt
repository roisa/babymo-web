Then came a sound: tick… tick… tick. Ais pulled the blanket up to her nose. “Umiii!”

Umi came straight away. “I'm here, sweetheart.” Ais hugged her tight. “There's a monster with long ears!”

Umi didn't laugh. “Being scared is okay. How about we become shadow detectives? Let's get the torch!”

Ais shone the torch at the wall. The long-eared shadow was really… the toy bunny on the shelf!

“And the tick-tick sound?” They searched… it was just the wall clock, busy working.

Ais giggled. “The monster was just a bunny and a clock!” Umi nodded. “The dark only makes ordinary things look strange.”
