🌙 Little Questions of Faith

"Who is Allah?" "Why do we pray?" "What is Paradise?" — gentle answers to a child's big questions.

📖 Start with book 1 →
  1. Book 1 ✓Who Is Allah?Baby Mo asks, "Umi, who is Allah?" So Umi takes him on a treasure hunt — to find the signs of Allah all around them.
  2. Book 2 ✓Who Created Me?In front of the mirror, Baby Mo asks, "Umi, who made me?" So they play "body explorer" — discovering how amazing Allah's creation is.
  3. Book 3 ✓Can Allah See Me?Playing hide-and-seek, Baby Mo hides in the darkest spot. "Nobody can see me!" he thinks. But… is that really true?
  4. Book 4 ✓Who Makes the Rain?As the rain falls, Baby Mo presses his face to the window. "Umi, who sends the rain?" Then he sees for himself how rain is Allah's mercy.
  5. Book 5 ✓Where Do We Go When We Sleep?Under the blanket, Baby Mo asks, "Umi, where do we go when we sleep?" And he learns that sleep is a gift of rest — and morning is the gift of a new day.
  6. Book 6 ✓Who Is Prophet Muhammad ﷺ?"Umi, who is Prophet Muhammad? Can I see him?" Baby Mo gets to know him in a fun way — by copying his kindness all day.
  7. Book 7 ✓What Is the Qur'an?Baby Mo watches Abi hold a book so carefully. "Umi, what book is that? Why is it so special?"
  8. Book 8 ✓What Are Angels?"Umi, what are angels? Why can't I see them?" Baby Mo learns there are kind, unseen friends — always writing down his good deeds.
  9. Book 9 ✓Why Do We Pray?Baby Mo watches Abi stop playing to pray. "Why do we pray, Abi?" Then Baby Mo finds the answer — with a little sujud of his own.
  10. Book 10 ✓Why Do We Wash Up Before Praying?"But my hands aren't dirty!" said Baby Ais. Then Abi handed her a magnifying glass — and a secret about the drops of wudhu water.
  11. Book 11 ✓What Is the Adhan?A beautiful voice drifts from far away, and Abi stops working at once. Baby Ais is curious — and discovers that the adhan is a call you can answer.
  12. Book 12 ✓Why Do We Fast?Baby Mo wants to fast like Abi. "But why do we hold back from food?" A half-day fast teaches him the answer.
  13. Book 13 ✓What Is Sadaqah?Two coins in his hand, one ice lolly he'd been dreaming of, and an old broom seller who hadn't sold a thing all day. Baby Mo has to choose.
  14. Book 14 ✓What Is Rezeki?While eating, Baby Mo asks, "Umi, where does this rice come from?" So they trace the journey of a grain of rice — all the way to its Giver.
  15. Book 15 ✓Why Be Honest?A cup breaks, and no one saw. Baby Mo almost lies — until he discovers that honesty makes the heart feel light.
  16. Book 16 ✓Why Should I Share?Baby Mo has two biscuits and doesn't want to share. But a little experiment teaches him a secret: happiness shared only grows.
  17. Book 17 ✓Why Do We Ask Permission?Baby Ais's red car was lying all alone, and Baby Mo just took it. So why did it feel so bad when Ais did the same thing back?
  18. Book 18 ✓Why Do We Love Our Parents?"Umi, why should I love and obey Umi and Abi?" Through one little mission, Baby Mo discovers just how big his parents' love is.
  19. Book 19 ✓What Is Jannah?"Umi, what is Jannah like?" asks Baby Mo. So they make a garden of good deeds — and Baby Mo learns how to plant his way there.
