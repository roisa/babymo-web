🌟 The Twins' Good Habits

Everyday routines made fun: the bathroom by yourself, brushing teeth, tidying toys, sleeping in your own bed, screen time and three magic words.

📖 Start with book 1 →
  1. Book 1 ✓Mo Can Go to the Bathroom by HimselfMo wants to use the bathroom by himself, like Kak Adam. Left foot first, say the du'a, don't forget to wash hands… so much to remember!
  2. Book 2 ✓Mo's Teeth and the Naughty GermsMo doesn't feel like brushing. “My teeth are already clean!” But that night, tiny germs are having a party between his teeth…
  3. Book 3 ✓Toys Go Home TooThe playroom is a mess: blocks, dolls and cars everywhere. Then Abi steps on something… OUCH!
  4. Book 4 ✓Baby Mo's Very Own BedMo's a big boy now, so he has his own bed. But in the middle of the night, it feels very big… and very lonely.
  5. Book 5 ✓The Tablet Needs a Rest Too“One more video! Just one!” Mo won't stop watching. Then the screen flickers… the battery's flat!
  6. Book 6 ✓Three Magic WordsMo discovers a secret: three words that make people smile, forgive and want to help. What could they be?
