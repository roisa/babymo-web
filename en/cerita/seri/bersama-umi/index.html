👩 With Umi

A burnt cake, the day Umi was poorly, little surprises — learning to love and help Umi.

📖 Start with book 1 →
  1. Book 1 ✓A Burnt Cake for UmiAbi, Kak Adam and the twins bake a cake for Umi. Flour flies, an egg drops… and the cake gets burnt!
  2. Book 2 ✓Little Chefs in Umi's KitchenToday the twins are little chefs! There's a hot-zone rule, carrots to count, an egg that goes — splat! — and a bowl of soup for the neighbour.
  3. Book 3 ✓Helping Umi Bake CookiesBaby Mo wants to help bake cookies. The flour goes everywhere, an egg smashes on the floor, and he's sure he'll be told off. But the kitchen has a surprise in store.
  4. Book 4 ✓The Day Umi Was SickThis morning Umi doesn't get up to make breakfast. She has a fever. Baby Mo and Baby Ais decide: today, they'll take care of Umi.
  5. Book 5 ✓A Day Just for UmiToday Umi gets a day off! Abi and the children take over all of Umi's jobs… and the list turns out to be very, very long.
  6. Book 6 ✓A Surprise for UmiBaby Mo and Baby Ais have a secret mission: to surprise Umi. But keeping a secret turns out to be very, very hard!
  7. Book 7 ✓Hugs Never Run OutA baby cousin comes to stay, and Umi holds her all day long. "I'm a baby too!" Baby Mo shouts. Can Umi's love run out?
  8. Book 8 ✓The Tablet Needs to Sleep Too"One more video, Umi!" When the sand in the hourglass runs out, the tablet has to sleep. Mo cries… and then finds the most fun day without a screen.
