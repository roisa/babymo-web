🦁 Brave Ais

Baby Ais learns to handle big feelings with Umi — anger, fear of the dark, the first day at kindergarten, and being ill.

📖 Start with book 1 →
  1. Book 1 ✓Ais Is Very, Very Cross!Mo knocks over Ais's block tower. Her face goes hot, her hands make fists… What do you do with a cross feeling that big?
  2. Book 2 ✓Ais and the Dark RoomThe light goes off, and the shadows on the wall start to look like monsters. Ais pulls the blanket up to her nose…
  3. Book 3 ✓Ais's First Day at KindergartenA new bag, new shoes, a new school… but Ais's tummy feels twisty. What if nobody wants to be her friend?
  4. Book 4 ✓Ais Has a FeverAis is hot all over and can't come and play. Mo really wants to help. But how do you look after someone who's ill?
