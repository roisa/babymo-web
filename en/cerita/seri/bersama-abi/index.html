👨 With Abi

The twins and Kak Adam with Abi: praying, morning exercise and a hug after work.

📖 Start with book 1 →
  1. Book 1 ✓Abi Comes HomeBaby Mo has been waiting for Abi since the afternoon. But when Abi gets home he's so tired… he falls asleep in the middle of the story!
  2. Book 2 ✓My First Sujud with AbiBaby Mo prays beside Abi. In sujud he doesn't want to get up — there's so much he wants to whisper!
  3. Book 3 ✓Straight Rows like a TrainPraying together at home! Abi leads, Kak Adam is in the front row. But Baby Mo can't stay still in his row…
  4. Book 4 ✓Abi's Magic WatchAbi keeps glancing at his watch and then suddenly stops working. Baby Mo is sure it's magic. What's the secret?
  5. Book 5 ✓Morning Exercise with AbiAfter Fajr, Abi gets everyone moving. Kak Adam wants to be fastest, Baby Mo is the slowest — but everyone gets there!
  6. Book 6 ✓The Day Abi Had a HeadacheAbi has a headache. The twins want to help — in "ant mode": tiptoeing, whispering, and leaving little gifts.
  7. Book 7 ✓Who Falls Asleep First?Abi's on bedtime duty tonight. But the story is too long… and the first one to fall asleep is Abi!
  8. Book 8 ✓Abi Goes Away for Three DaysAbi has to work in another city for three days. Kak Adam promises to look after the little ones. Can he do it?
  9. Book 9 ✓Baby Mo Washes Abi's CarAbi's car is covered in mud, and Baby Mo wants to help. But those soap bubbles are so tempting…
  10. Book 10 ✓The Friday Charity BoxEvery Friday, Kak Adam drops a coin in the charity box. Baby Mo wants to join in — but he has one coin and he wants sweets!
  11. Book 11 ✓Learning Iqra with Kak AdamKak Adam can read the Qur'an fluently. Baby Mo wants to as well — but the letters all look alike! Alif, ba, ta… tha?
