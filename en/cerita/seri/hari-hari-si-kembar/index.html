🎒 The Twins' Everyday Adventures

School, the ice-cream shop, the mosque and outings — Mo and Ais's little adventures, full of good manners and love.

📖 Start with book 1 →
  1. Book 1 ✓A New Friend in a New ClassBaby Mo is moving up to a bigger class. But that morning his feet felt heavy — what if no one wants to be his friend?
  2. Book 2 ✓One Lunchbox, Three FriendsMo forgot his lunchbox! Ais shares hers. Then they spot Dimas sitting alone with nothing to eat. Can one lunchbox be enough for three?
  3. Book 3 ✓The Long Ice Cream LineTen people ahead of Baby Mo, and his chocolate ice cream feels as far away as the moon. But it turns out waiting can be filled with a secret game.
  4. Book 4 ✓Three Scoops of Ice CreamAt the ice-cream shop, Mo wants three scoops at once: chocolate, strawberry AND vanilla! He eats as fast as lightning… then — OUCH! His head goes freezing and his tummy is far too full.
  5. Book 5 ✓First Time at the MasjidBaby Mo goes with Abi to pray Maghrib at the masjid for the very first time. It's big, the carpet is red, and everyone is quiet. Can Baby Mo be calm too?
  6. Book 6 ✓Bismillah FirstBaby Mo is always in a hurry. Until he discovers one little word that makes everything feel more blessed.
  7. Book 7 ✓Sharing the UmbrellaThere is only one umbrella and two children. The rain is pouring. What will Baby Mo do?
