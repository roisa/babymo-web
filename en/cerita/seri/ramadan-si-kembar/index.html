🏮 The Twins' Ramadan

From the first lantern to Eid morning — sahur, a half-day fast, a forgotten drink, the iftar market, first hijaiyah letters, tarawih, Laylatul Qadr, zakat and the night of takbir with Mo and Ais.

📖 Start with book 1 →
  1. Book 1 ✓The Twins' Ramadan LightsAbi takes the twins to look for the thinnest crescent moon in the sky. If they see it, Ramadan starts tomorrow! Then out comes a box full of magical lights…
  2. Book 2 ✓The Twins' Half-Day FastToday Mo and Ais are fasting until the noon drum. The morning feels easy… until Mo's tummy starts to rumble and the fridge looks very tempting.
  3. Book 3 ✓Mo Forgot He Was FastingOn a hot afternoon, Mo is so thirsty after football — gulp, gulp, gulp! Then Ais shouts, "MO! You're fasting!" Has Mo broken his fast?
  4. Book 4 ✓Does Mo's Breath Smell of Musk?"Eww, Mo, your breath smells!" says Ais, holding her nose. Mo panics — is he ill? But the Prophet ﷺ had surprising news about the breath of someone who fasts…
  5. Book 5 ✓Waiting for the Sunset DrumToday the twins are fasting all the way to sunset! While they wait, they make banana kolak with Umi — half for the family, half to share. Who will it go to?
  6. Book 6 ✓The Twins at the Iftar MarketAbi gives the twins ten thousand rupiah at the iftar market. Ais buys just enough. Hungry Mo wants EVERYTHING! Can Mo's little tummy finish it all?
  7. Book 7 ✓One Letter, One JuzRamadan is the month of the Qur'an. Kak Adam wants to finish all thirty juz! Mo wants to join in… but the letters are "all curly". What if he starts with just one letter?
  8. Book 8 ✓Baby Mo's First TarawihThe first night of Ramadan! Mo wants to pray tarawih at the mosque all the way to the end, like Kak Adam. But there are so many rakaat, and his eyes keep getting heavier…
  9. Book 9 ✓The Laylatul Qadr DetectivesOne night in Ramadan is better than a thousand months — but which one? Detective Mo and Detective Ais are ready to crack Ramadan's biggest mystery!
  10. Book 10 ✓The Twins' Night of TakbirRamadan is nearly over, and Ais is sad to see it go. But there's one important job before Eid: taking the zakat rice to the mosque. Then comes a night of torches and takbir!
