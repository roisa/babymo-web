36:T69f,After Fajr prayer, the sky was still orange. Abi tied his shoelaces. "Who wants to exercise?"

"ME!" Kak Adam already had his shoes on. Baby Mo had one shoe on… his hand.

At the park, Abi led the warm-up. "Arms up… stretch to the side… like a tree in the wind!"

Baby Ais yawned hugely as she stretched. "Hooaam… I'm still half asleep!" Everyone laughed.

"Now run round the park!" Kak Adam shot off in front. Whoosh!

Abi ran in the middle. Baby Ais behind him. And Baby Mo… right at the back, on his short legs: pitter-patter-pitter.

"Waaait!" Baby Mo panted. "I'm the slowest. I don't want to run any more!"

Kak Adam stopped, then ran back. "Come on, Mo! Let's run together. I'll be a tortoise too!"

They jogged slowly side by side, counting: "One, two, Bismillah! Three, four, Alhamdulillah!"

The finish line was the big tree. Everyone made it — fast ones and slow ones. "Hooray!"

Then they sat on the grass and drank water slowly — sitting down, in three sips — the way the Prophet ﷺ did.

"Why do we have to exercise, Abi?" asked Baby Ais. "Because our bodies are a gift Allah has trusted us with. We look after them."

> *"The strong believer is better and more beloved to Allah than the weak believer, and there is good in both." (Muslim)*

"Being strong isn't just running fast," Abi added. "Waiting for a slower little one takes a strong heart too." Kak Adam smiled shyly.

Breakfast was fresh fruit. Baby Mo ate his banana fastest of all. "Look! I'm the banana champion!"

From then on, every Sunday morning they ran together. Kak Adam always stayed beside Baby Mo.
