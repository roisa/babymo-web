Baby Mo sat listening, chin in his hands. "Kak, I want to read like you!"

Abi smiled. "Kak Adam started with Iqra book one too. Would you like to be your brother's teacher, Kak?"

"This is ba — like a boat with one dot underneath. Ba!" Baby Mo pointed. "A boat! Ba!"

"Ta — the boat gets two dots on top. Tha — three dots!" Baby Mo frowned. "Why are all the boats the same?"

On day two, Baby Mo mixed up ba and ta. On day three, he forgot tha. He closed the book. "It's hard, Kak!"
