SahihHR. Muslim 234

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ

Dua After Ablution

Recited immediately after completing wudu — the Prophet ﷺ promised that the eight gates of paradise open for the one who says it.
